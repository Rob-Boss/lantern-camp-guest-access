♨️ Sauna Hours & Guidelines

🔐 Private Session Window (7am – 5pm)

Bookable private 50-minute sessions for your cabin (booked by primary guest). All cabin guests and invited Lantern Camp guests are welcome to join (no off-site guests). Please vacate at 10 minutes to the hour.

Clothing optional (must sit on a clean towel on wooden benches).
👥 Open Communal Hours (5pm Onwards)

Open access for all registered camp guests to share and enjoy together without reservations needed.

Bathing suit or modest towel covering required.
  • Automated Preheat: The cedarwood sauna preheats and cycles off automatically. If you believe it's malfunctioning, please immediately contact a staff member by text at (207) 560-6717.
  • Hydration & Bench Care: Please drink plenty of water before and after your session, and always place a clean towel beneath you on the wooden benches.
  • Safety Regulations: Glass containers, alcohol, smoking, and open flames are strictly prohibited inside the sauna structure.
